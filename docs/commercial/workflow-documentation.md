# Phase 5 — Commercial & Financial Workflows

## 1. Quote-to-Cash (Sales Lifecycle)
1. **Customer & Tax Exemption Setup:** Customers record payment terms (`payment_terms`), credit limits, and optional `reseller_permits`. Customers with an active reseller permit are automatically exempted from VAT calculation on invoices and sales receipts.
2. **Estimates / Quotations (`#824` RBAC):** Estimates store line-level `unit_cost` and `total_cost` for margin analysis. Server-side redaction strips `unitCost`, `totalCost`, and `estimatedProfit` for `Accountant` and `Read-Only` roles while preserving full costing for `Administrator`, `Bookkeeper`, and `ProductionManager`.
3. **Convert to Invoice / Direct Invoicing:** Converting an estimate or posting an invoice validates against `raw_material` invoicing (`400 RAW_MATERIAL_NOT_INVOICEABLE`), enforces customer `credit_limit` (`409`), deducts perpetual inventory (`stationery` directly; `printed_product`/`printing_service` via BOM raw material `conversion_factor` consumption), and posts balanced AR/Revenue/VAT/COGS/Inventory entries.
4. **Sales Receipts:** Point-of-sale transactions (`is_sales_receipt = true`) debit the chosen Bank/Cash GL account directly and insert a posted `bank_transactions` register row.
5. **Customer Payments, Overpayments & Credit Memos:** Payments support multi-invoice allocations (`payment_allocations`). Any excess over allocated invoices remains as unapplied customer credit in AR (`customers.balance` reflects net open AR less unapplied credits/memos). Credit memos reduce customer balance and apply against open invoices via `credit_applications`.
6. **Recurring Invoices & Collections:** `POST /api/recurring-invoices/run-due` uses deterministic `idempotencyKey` (`rec-inv-{companyId}-{recId}-{nextDue}`) and advances `next_due`, guaranteeing repeated runs on the same day never double-bill. Overdue reminders log to `invoice_reminders` and `email_log`.

## 2. Procure-to-Pay (Purchasing Lifecycle)
1. **Purchase Orders & Receiving:** Purchase Orders track ordered vs `received_qty` per line. Receiving (`POST /api/purchase-orders/:id/receive`) supports partial and full receipts, automatically creating a linked vendor Bill and transitioning PO status (`open` -> `partial` -> `closed`).
2. **Unit Conversion & Perpetual Costing:** Stocked `raw_material` items received on Bills/POs multiply purchase quantity by `conversion_factor` (e.g., `1 Ream -> 500 Sheets`), record an `inventory_movements` purchase row, and recalculate `avg_cost` in exact `BigInt` arithmetic.
3. **Duplicate Protection & Use Tax:** `uq_bills_company_vendor_num` prevents duplicate vendor bill numbers (`409`). Bill lines support `use_tax_rate` accrual to `2200`.
4. **Bill Payments & Vendor Credits:** Bill payments allocate across multiple open bills, auto-assign sequential `next_check_number` on linked bank accounts, support check print queuing, and post bank register entries. Vendor credits apply cleanly against open bills.

## 3. Cash, Banking & Reconciliation
1. **Checks, Split Deposits & Paired Transfers:** Checks can be queued with `printLater = true` and batch-marked printed via `/api/banking/checks/print`. Inter-account transfers create a single balanced GL entry and two cross-linked `bank_transactions` rows (`transfer_pair_txn_id`).
2. **CSV/OFX Bank Statement Import & Rules:** `POST /api/banking/imports` parses CSV rows or OFX `<STMTTRN>` blocks, deduplicates by `(company_id, account_id, external_fitid)`, evaluates prioritized `bank_rules` (`payee`, `memo`, `amount`), and optionally auto-posts matched items to the GL (`autoApplyRules: true`).
3. **Bank Reconciliation Lock & Audited Reversal:**
   - Preview computes `beginningBalance + clearedDeposits - clearedPayments` and `difference`.
   - Completion enforces `difference === 0n` (`400 RECONCILIATION_UNBALANCED`, reinforced by PostgreSQL `CHECK (difference = 0.0000)`), links `bank_transactions.reconciliation_id`, and sets `journal_lines.is_reconciled = TRUE`.
   - Voiding a reconciled bank transaction or reversing its GL journal is blocked (`409 RECONCILED_ENTRY_LOCKED`) until `POST /api/banking/reconciliations/:id/reverse` is executed with a mandatory audit reason.

## 4. Foreign Exchange, Budgets, Tax & Saved Reports
- **Foreign Exchange:** Documents created in a non-base currency convert amounts via `exchange_rates` (or explicit `exchangeRate`) and record `foreign_total` / `foreign_balance_due`. Settlement at a different FX rate automatically posts realized FX Gain (`4900`) or FX Loss (`6900`) while relieving AR/AP at the original booking rate.
- **Budgets & Variance:** 12-month account budget matrices (`budgets`) compare against live GL net movement (`getBudgetVsActualReport`) with Favorable/Unfavorable status indicators.
- **Tax & Saved Reports:** Sales & Use Tax summaries reconcile output VAT (`invoices`), VAT reversals (`credit_memos`), and accrued purchase use tax (`bill_lines`) against GL `2200`. Saved report presets persist filter parameters and execute on demand (`POST /api/saved-reports/:id/run`).

## 5. Finance Controls & Period Close (Phase 11)
- **Maker-Checker Approvals:** Per-company `approval_policies` define action types (`invoice`, `bill`, `customer_payment`, `bill_payment`, `journal_entry`, `credit_memo`) and monetary thresholds. When a threshold is met, the creation route returns `202` with an `approval_requests` row and does not create the financial document. A different user with `approvals:decide` approves or rejects; self-approval is rejected with `409 SELF_APPROVAL_FORBIDDEN`. The client retries the original creation with `approvalRequestId`; the request is atomically marked consumed and linked to the created document.
- **Period Close Checklist:** `GET /api/periods/:id/checklist` derives automatic evidence for trial balance balance, bank reconciliation, AR/AP/inventory subledger-to-GL ties, and pending approvals, and adds manual tasks for accruals, FX revaluation, recurring invoice review, and management sign-off. `POST /api/periods/:id/checklist/:taskKey/complete` records manual completion or a reasoned waiver. When `period_close_requires_checklist` is enabled, close/soft-close is blocked with `409 CLOSE_CHECKLIST_INCOMPLETE` until all required tasks are satisfied.
- **Operational Reporting:** Dedicated endpoints expose printing job profitability, WIP aging, spoilage/waste, estimate-vs-actual, inventory valuation, and COGS-to-subledger reconciliation (`/api/reports/printing-job-profitability`, `/wip-aging`, `/printing-spoilage`, `/estimate-vs-actual`, `/inventory-valuation`, `/cogs-reconciliation`).
- **Scheduled Delivery & Alerts:** `report_schedules` support daily/weekly/monthly/quarterly runs with recipient lists, filters, run history (`report_schedule_runs`), and queued delivery records in `email_log`. `GET /api/dashboard/alerts` surfaces pending approvals, overdue receivables, low stock, unreconciled bank items, WIP over budget, stuck jobs, and disabled approval policies.
